Questions

Do I need my own API keys?

On the Free plan and any paid plan you can bring your own keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google, Groq, Mistral) and store them in an encrypted vault — you pay your provider directly at cost, with no Analytikul markup on tokens.

How is agent cost controlled?

Every agent run shows live token and dollar cost, and you can set budget caps that automatically pause runs before they exceed a limit — per user and org-wide on Team.

Can I self-host?

Yes. Enterprise supports self-hosting and a dedicated/on-prem agent runtime, with SSO, audit logging and a security review.

When does billing go live?

Billing connects to Stripe shortly. Until then the workspace is open to early users, and founding accounts keep early pricing when billing launches.
